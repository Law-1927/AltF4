Using the booking service

Provide accurate contact, vehicle, and booking information. A booking is subject to the availability and confirmation shown in your account. Keep your reference number and review your booking details before the appointment.

Changes and cancellation

Pending bookings may be cancelled through the client booking-history page. Contact Shimmer and Shield directly for help with a booking that cannot be cancelled online. Any final cancellation, late-arrival, payment, warranty, and service terms must be supplied and approved by the business before production use.
